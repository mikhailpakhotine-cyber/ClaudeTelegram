About This Project

This public humanities project makes the philosophical dimensions of Atomic Heart accessible to academic researchers through interactive storytelling and multimedia presentations. The project examines how the game engages with profound questions about consciousness, human enhancement, and the Soviet philosophical tradition of scientism.
